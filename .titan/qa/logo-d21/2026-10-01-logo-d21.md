# LensSpace logo D2.1 audit and refinement

Date: 2026-10-01

## Audit of D2 (logo-design skill)

- `svg_audit.py`: horizontal lockups FAIL on live `<text>` (score 65); they
  rendered in Arial on machines without Space Grotesk. 7 colours per symbol.
- Pixel test: at 16 px the split ring, axis and lids merged into one blob.
- Zoom: round caps of the ring arcs protruded beside the axis (junction bumps).
- Right ring half `#0D7A72` had ~2.7:1 contrast on the `#07322F` tile versus
  ~6.3:1 for the left half; it read as half a ring at small sizes.
- No untiled single-ink symbol existed.

## D2.1 changes

- Ring arcs use butt caps that end under the axis.
- Right ring half uses palette `#128F84` (~3.5:1).
- Removed the `#FFD9D0` highlight (invisible below 48 px).
- New `lensspace-mark-small.svg` for 16-32 px (thicker lids, single ring, no
  axis); used by `src/app/icon.svg`.
- Wordmark in both horizontal lockups outlined from Space Grotesk Bold
  (audit score 89, no FAIL).
- New `lensspace-symbol-ink-dark.svg` / `-ink-white.svg` without tile.
- PNG icons (192, 512, apple 180) regenerated from the app-icon SVG.
